UPDATE LOG

This game is an unfinished beta. Things will change, break and get added.

v0.8.0 BETA (current)

  • Level 9: Haven (creator-made). A suburb under a fake dome where night falls every 30 seconds and the eyes come. Houses are safe, but the residents are not.
  • Multiplayer: up to 6 players (PC and VR together) via room codes, a shared waiting room, host migration, voice + text chat, ghosts and revives, shared exits, and per-player shops and loot.
  • Offloaded asset loading: when served over http(s), audio, rigs and animations are loaded after the page itself, one at a time, from the parent folders (audio / rigs / animations) or from an assets.txt. Opened as a file, the embedded base64 is used as before.
  • Bacteria: the freeze is now a random ability that needs a bacteriaability animation; touching it is a normal jumpscare. Fixed the lag spike when pressing Next Level.

v0.7.0 BETA

  • VR mode with physical item interactions, plus two-handed shotguns with recoil, visible bullet paths and muzzle flashes. Fixed VR crate/chest collision sizing and the QWERTY debug-room teleport in VR.
  • Loot chests with randomized loot pools, breakable crates opened with crowbars, and the Chest Locator. Chest/crate spawn rates were retuned and rare floor loot was added.
  • Sub-levels (0.2, 1.5) are now secret branches: they are only reachable through hidden, hard-to-spot entrances in dead ends, not as the normal next level. Their exits rejoin the main path.
  • Thalassophobia reworked into an open ocean (dark rock, sea stacks, depth layers, light shafts). The Leviathan now hunts swimmers and can kill. Electrical Station lag fixes and general performance improvements.

v0.6.0 BETA

  • Alarm clocks ring once. Being jumpscared, dying or pausing silences them.
  • Master, music, jumpscare and SFX volume sliders. Every setting now saves between sessions.
  • Monsters turn at a limited speed instead of snapping to face you (Smilers too).
  • Chases no longer flicker on and off. You stay hunted while anything is still tracking your last known position.
  • Smilers drain sanity much more slowly.
  • New rounded HUD, plus film grain, tracking glitches, a rolling bar, CRT glow and a very quiet tape hiss.

v0.5.0 BETA

  • Smarter monsters: they chase your last known position, hear footsteps and loud noises (not footsteps on Easy), investigate sounds, and wander for real.
  • Only one monster hunts you at a time, and only one lurks near you.
  • Textures now have embossed patterns and depth. Random furniture and clutter appear in levels.
  • Party tables are hiding spots. Partygoers that see you duck under will smash the table.
  • Alarm clocks can appear in any level. Wind one up, throw it, and every monster sprints to it. Holding it is a bad idea.
  • Main menu monsters are easier to see.

v0.4.0 BETA

  • All monster models upgraded: glowing pulsing eyes, tendrils, bone claws, spine spikes and extra watching eyes, all of which get more frantic while they hunt you.
  • Chases now hit harder: a heartbeat-tempo red pulse, thicker pulsing fog, camera shake and an FOV kick.
  • A new secret is out there somewhere.

v0.3.1 BETA

  • Party Room (BETA) now has arcade cabinets with a tiny endless platformer. Playing restores sanity, but you are NOT safe while playing.
  • Aggressive partygoers now roam the Party Room, and it is more tightly packed.
  • Smilers rebuilt: bright floating grins that always face you, red/cyan 3D-glasses fringe, and they fade in out of the dark.
  • Shotguns no longer vanish. They stay in your inventory unloaded. Find shotgun shells to reload; a rare AMMO CRATE refills every shotgun with 2 shots.
  • Press Q to drop the selected item.
  • Every area now has its own ambience and footstep sound slots (see LOAD SOUND FILES).
  • Textures: stains, scratches and edge grime added across the whole game.

v0.3.0 BETA

  • Added the Party Room (BETA): party walls, floors, tables and dancing partygoers, not available by normal means yet.
  • Party Cakes spawn only in the Party Room. [E] takes one, [C] eats it. Heals a hit, or grants one extra hit at full health.
  • Textures: fine grain added. Main menu: entities lurk behind the Bacteria, flickering title, tips, update log.
